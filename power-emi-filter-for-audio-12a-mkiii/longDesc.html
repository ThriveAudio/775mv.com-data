Engineering Excellence

Every aspect of the Elite-MKIII has been optimized for audio applications:

Common-Mode Filtering

High-inductance transformers provide exceptional common-mode EMI suppression, eliminating interference that travels on both power conductors simultaneously. The majority of competitive products tend to also provide this, but only this mode of EMI suppression.

Differential Filtering

Dual high-inductance high-saturation-current coils combined with premium metal-film polypropylene capacitors create a strong barrier against differential EMI; the type of nuisance that becomes more and more prevalent in our houses with proliferation of SMPS (Switching Mode Power Supplies) - computers, chargers, home appliances, even LED light bulbs, those offenders are countless in a modern household.

Ground Loop Breaking with High Current Fault Protection

Powerful diodes safely break ground loops while careful dampening prevents resonance effects. Fault current handling capability exceeds 250A. The Ground Loop Breaking circuit bypass capacitors can be selected for optimising the further common mode EMI suppression provided by filtering in the load.

Low-Impedance Design

Optimized PCB layout with premium fuses and terminals ensures minimal resistance for unrestricted dynamic performance.
