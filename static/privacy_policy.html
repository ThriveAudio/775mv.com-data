Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs how Thrive Audio LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, stores, uses, and discloses information about users of 775mv.com (the "Site"). This policy applies to the Site, along with related products and services offered by Thrive Audio LLC that link to this policy. This policy does not apply to websites, applications, or services that display or link to different privacy statements.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you create an account or make a purchase on our Site, we collect and store the following information in your user account:

  • Email address (which also serves as your user ID)
  • Order history
  • Shipping and billing addresses
  • Phone number

Payment Information

For your security, we do not store sensitive payment information including:

  • Credit card numbers
  • Expiration dates
  • CVV codes

This payment data is processed directly by our payment integrators (Authorize.Net and/or PayPal.com) and is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Password Security

We follow industry best security practices for password protection. We do not store your actual passwords on our servers. Instead, we store only hashed and salted data that allows us to authenticate users while keeping your password secure.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses web cookies solely to maintain a unique session ID that allows for continuity between your browsing sessions. We do not store any other cookies on your device through our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Shipping products to you
  • Contacting you regarding order status or shipping issues
  • Providing customer service and support
  • Sending occasional updates about new products and features (no more than one email per month under normal conditions)

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not, and will never, sell or give any of your personal information to third parties. Your information is kept strictly confidential and is used only for the purposes outlined in this policy.

The only exception is when we work with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business, such as:

  • Payment processors (Authorize.Net and PayPal.com)
  • Shipping companies (to deliver your orders)

These service providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and are only permitted to use your information for the specific services they provide to us.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can access and review your account information at any time by logging into your account
  • Update: You can update your personal information through your account settings
  • Email Communications: You can opt out of promotional emails by following the unsubscribe link in any promotional email or by contacting us directly
  • Account Deletion: You can request deletion of your account by contacting us

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. We will also retain and use your information as necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
