Lifetime Defect Warranty

Beyond our satisfaction guarantee, we provide lifetime coverage against manufacturing defects for the entire useful life of the product.

Coverage: Manufacturing defects and component failures under normal use
Duration: Lifetime of the product
Condition: Defects must not be caused by misuse, abuse, or normal wear and tear

Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) Process

Important: All returns require prior authorization. Do not ship products back without an RMA number.

How to Obtain RMA:

  1. Contact us through our website: https://775mv.com/contact
  2. Provide your order number and description of the issue
  3. We will issue an RMA number and return shipping instructions
  4. Follow the provided return shipping address and method exactly

RMA Requirements:

  • RMA number must be clearly marked on the outside of the return package
  • Product must be packaged securely to prevent damage during shipping
  • Include original accessories and documentation when possible
  • Customer is responsible for return shipping costs unless otherwise specified

Replacement Terms

When a warranty claim is approved, we will provide a replacement product that meets or exceeds the performance of the original item.

Replacement Options:

  • Same model (subject to availability)
  • Newer model with similar or better performance characteristics
  • Functionally equivalent alternative

Important Notice: Due to ongoing product development and component availability, we cannot guarantee that replacement modules will have identical physical dimensions to the original product. We will make every effort to provide dimensionally compatible replacements when possible.

Refund Terms

When a refund is approved, the refund amount will be limited to the actual price paid by the customer for the specific product being returned.

Shipping Cost Refunds:

  • If the returned product was the only item in the original order, we will also refund the original shipping costs
  • If multiple products were purchased in the same order, shipping costs will not be refunded unless all products from that order are being returned

We are not responsible for refunding any additional charges collected from the customer by third parties, including but not limited to import taxes, customs duties, handling fees, or any other government-imposed charges. These charges are the responsibility of the customer and will not be refunded under any circumstances.
